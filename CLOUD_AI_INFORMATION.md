# Cloud AI Information

## What Cloud AI Means
Cloud AI refers to artificial intelligence services that run on cloud infrastructure and can be accessed over the internet.

## Core Characteristics
- On-demand access to AI models and compute resources
- Scalable processing for small and large workloads
- Managed infrastructure and regular platform updates
- API-based integration with applications

## Common Cloud AI Capabilities
- Natural language processing (chatbots, summarization, translation)
- Computer vision (image classification, object detection)
- Predictive analytics and forecasting
- Speech recognition and text-to-speech

## Benefits
- Faster development with prebuilt AI services
- Reduced operational overhead
- Flexible pricing models based on usage
- Easier collaboration across distributed teams

## Considerations
- Data privacy and compliance requirements
- Model bias and responsible AI practices
- Latency and regional deployment needs
- Cost monitoring and usage governance

## Typical Use Cases
- Customer support assistants
- Fraud detection and risk scoring
- Document processing and classification
- Personalized recommendations
